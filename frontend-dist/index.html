Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding student rights, grievance filing, and confidential whistleblower protections.

🕶️ Can I file a complaint completely anonymously? ▼

YES! When creating a complaint, simply check "Submit Anonymously 🕶️". The BU Privacy Gate strips your name, student ID, and email from the public board and API responses. Even other students will only see "Anonymous Student 🕶️".

🔒 What is the difference between Public Notice and Confidential Vault? ▼

Public complaints appear on the main campus bulletin board where fellow students can vote "+1 Me Too" to raise visibility. Confidential Vault complaints (such as ragging, harassment, or sensitive disputes) are completely sealed and dispatched exclusively to the Proctor Office triage desk.

👍 How does the "+1 Me Too" upvote system work? ▼

If an issue also affects you (like a broken water purifier or delayed bus), click "+1 Me Too". Issues with more upvotes automatically rise to the top of the priority queue for faster department resource allocation.

⏱️ What is the average resolution time for campus complaints? ▼

For urgent safety and electrical issues, response time is under 2 hours. Routine classroom and hostel repairs are typically resolved within 24–48 hours, monitored strictly by university administration.

🚨 What should I do if I am facing immediate ragging or safety threats? ▼

Immediately dial the 24/7 Proctor Hotline at +880-1700-BU-HELP or contact your Hall Warden. You can also file an emergency ticket with priority "Urgent" which triggers an instant alert on the proctor dashboard.
