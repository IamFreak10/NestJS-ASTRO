⚡ Fullstack Dataflow Diagram

From student submission to proctor review, field technician dispatch, and verified resolution.

1

Student Intake

Student browses public board in Astro. Can either upvote (+1) an existing issue or file a new ticket with the anonymous switch.

Astro SSR (Port 4321)
2

NestJS Security Triage

Better Auth validates session. Privacy Gate strips identity for anonymous complaints and seals private grievances into the Proctor Vault.

AuthGuard & RolesGuard
3

Staff Field Action

Department head assigns issue to electrician/warden. Technician posts progress notes in complaint_comments.

Status: "in_progress"
4

Verified Resolution

Proctorial verification verifies the repair. Official resolution note is recorded and live badge flips to "RESOLVED ✔️".

Status: "resolved"
